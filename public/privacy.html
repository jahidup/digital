Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 02, 2026. Review our data practices.

At Sankalp Digital Pathshala (operating under the Sankalp Shiksha Foundation), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us on our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit a contact inquiry, fill out the student enrollment form, or submit queries through our AI assistant widgets. This information includes your name, class level, location, phone number, and guardian details.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for offering counseling support, scheduling demo batches, and validating academic performance cards. We do not sell or lease student records to external marketing systems.

3. AI Services & Storage

When you upload file doubt queries (images or PDFs), they are processed in real-time by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and OpenRouter. When administrators upload student result records, their photos are stored securely using Cloudinary servers.

4. Cookies and Security

We use session cookies only to verify administrator logins. We protect our APIs using Zod schemas, CORS, and rate limits to block malicious requests.

5. Contact Details

For questions relating to privacy or database data deletion requests, contact us at info@sankalppathshala.com.
