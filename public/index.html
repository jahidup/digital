We Do Not Just Teach.
We Shape Thinkers.

Established under the Sankalp Shiksha Foundation, Sankalp Digital Pathshala has redefined the coaching standard in Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh since 2020. We believe true development happens when rigorous offline classroom discipline merges with artificial intelligence-driven personal reinforcement.

Every student is unique, and a crowded classroom can sometimes feel overwhelming. By limiting our physical batch size to 25 students, our expert faculty (comprising IITians, NITians, and Ph.D. scholars) interacts with each child individually. After classes, our 24/7 AI doubt solver and customized test analyses ensure that student self-study hours at home are highly targeted and productive.
