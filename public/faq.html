Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about admissions, classroom processes, mock test streams, and our AI support systems.

Where is Sankalp Digital Pathshala located?

+
Our campus is located at Salemgarh, Tamkuhi, Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh - 274407. The facility is equipped with smart classrooms, science labs, a library, and an innovation lab.

Who can enroll in the programs?

+
We offer structured classroom courses for middle school (Classes 6 – 8), board preparatory tracks (Classes 9 – 10), higher secondary streams (Classes 11 – 12 for PCM, PCB, Commerce, and Humanities), and specialized engineering (JEE) & medical (NEET) entrance prep. We also offer weekend coding and robotics labs.

How do the AI tools support students?

+
We provide access to a 24/7 AI Doubt Solver (supporting text query, photo uploads, and PDF scan uploads) and our conversational AI mentor, Sankalp Sathi. These tools help students during self-study hours at home to resolve academic doubts instantly in Hindi, English, and Hinglish.

What is the average batch size in classes?

+
To ensure every student gets personalized attention, we strictly restrict our offline batches to a maximum of 25 students.

Do you offer test series for boards and entrance exams?

+
Yes. We conduct weekly chapter-wise evaluation tests and full 3-hour mock test modules matching exact board and competitive (JEE CBT, NEET OMR) patterns. All results are indexed in our AI student analytics dashboard.

How can I verify my exam marksheet?

+
You can check and download your academic results at our public verification page. Simply enter your student registration code and date of birth to display a verified, printable report card containing your official photo.

Is transport assistance available for local villages?

+
Yes. We offer coordinated transport arrangements covering Salemgarh, Tamkuhi Raj, Seorahi, and nearby village pickup routes. Please discuss transport options with the admissions desk during your campus visit.

What are the offline class timings?

+
Classroom programs run in morning and evening shifts. Morning shifts run from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, and evening shifts run from 2:30 PM to 7:30 PM. Exact schedules depend on the student's grade and target exam track.

Do you offer scholarship or fee concessions?

+
Yes, the Sankalp Shiksha Foundation offers scholarship concessions of up to 90% based on student performance in our offline diagnostic tests, as well as concessions for families demonstrating financial need.

Can students access the Robotics Lab outside class?

+
Students enrolled in our Future Skills modules can access the Robotics and Innovation Lab during supervised weekend open hours (Saturdays 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM) to work on hardware and programming projects.

What is your refund policy?

+
We offer a full refund of tuition fees within the first 7 days of course commencement if a student decides not to proceed with enrollment. Please refer to our terms sheet for detailed conditions.

Who can I contact for technical support with AI tools?

+
For issues with AI Doubt Solver uploads, streaming chat, or student dashboard access, contact our technical support team at +91 8055698328 or support@sankalppathshala.com.
