Foundation

Foundation (Classes 6 – 8)

Our Middle School Foundation program focuses on helping students build strong logical thinking and scientific inquiry habits. It prepares students for high school board exams and national Olympiads.

Core Subjects & Topics:
  • Mathematics: Integers, Fractions, Rational Numbers, Data Handling, Mensuration, Introduction to Algebra
  • Sciences: Nutrition, Heat, Acids & Bases, Light, Electricity, Cell Structures, Force & Pressure
  • English: Grammar Syntaxes, Active/Passive Voice, Reading Comprehension, Essay and Composition Writing
  • Social Sciences: Indian History, Geographical Maps, Civics and Governance Principles
  • Introductory Coding: Basic logical loops, block coding (Scratch), and algorithmic thinking

Schedule: 4 Days/week | 2.5 Hours/day | Monthly Mock Tests
