Our Testing Streams

Offline testing conducted under real exam constraints, followed by detailed performance diagnostic reports.

📝 Board Mock Exams

Designed for Classes 10 and 12, our board mock exams help students practice formatting descriptive answers, managing their time, and writing under standard exam conditions.

🖥️ JEE Main & Advanced CBT

Our Computer-Based Testing (CBT) platform simulates the actual JEE environment, helping engineering aspirants build confidence and improve their question-solving speed.

🧬 NEET Medical Mocks

Intensive OMR-sheet practice sessions designed to prepare students for the NEET structure. Focuses on speed and accuracy across biology, chemistry, and physics sections.

🏆 School Olympiads (NTSE/SOF)

Testing modules focusing on logical reasoning, mental ability, and advanced mathematics, designed for early Olympiad exam preparation.

AI-Driven Performance Diagnostics

We index all student test scores in our evaluation dashboard. Beyond simple numerical grades, our system tracks:

  • Topic-wise Accuracy: Highlights specific chapters and subtopics that need revision.
  • Time Distribution: Measures solving speed to help students allocate time effectively during exams.
  • Benchmarked Ranks: Simulates national percentile projections based on peer group scores.
